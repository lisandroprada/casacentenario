# Casa Centenario — surface brief

Scope: index.html, one-page brochure. Mode: Persuade. Audience, job and proof: see PRODUCT.md (mixed buyers, mobile-first, action = WhatsApp). Memorable moment: the lot drawn as a plan, its dimension lines drawing themselves in.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The house read as a set of drawings (juego de planos). Each section is a sheet with a title block; the property is shown the way its municipal plans show it, redrawn and cleaned of owner data. Refuses the real-estate default: giant photo, price badge, equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched Propietas azul (#2e3092) ground with white linework and celeste (#27aae1) dimension lines, alternating with white sheets in azul ink for the data and the photos. Montserrat only: Black caps for titles, Light for body, tabular numerals for dimensions. Photos are framed "vistas" with hairline keylines and a caption in the title block. Planilla de locales as a ruled table. No grid texture, no gradients.

STORY: In seconds: casa con local comercial, Playa Unión, a 100 m del mar, USD 140.000. Then proof: real photos and the honest plan (lote 9,90 × 30,00 m, 297 m²). Then action: WhatsApp, always one tap away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile 390 wide. Top bar: white Propietas logo (>=120px) left, WhatsApp pill right. Below, the lot as a drawn plan filling the width: frente sobre Av. Centenario at the bottom, cotas 9,90 and 30,00 drawn in celeste, schematic zones (local, vivienda, galería, parque) labeled, noted "esquema ilustrativo, no a escala". Title "Casa con local en Playa Unión" in Black caps, then a title block row: Centenario 437 / USD 140.000 / a ~100 m del mar, and the primary WhatsApp button.

FORM: Redrawn work plan (plano de obra), pick card, seed key 1f156d2e, position 1 of 7 on my ordered list (assigned was 5, user chose the pick).
